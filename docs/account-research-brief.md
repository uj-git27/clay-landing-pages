**Likely problems for the role (proposed)**

**Use cases to show (proposed)**

2. Build a use-case matched account list from public signals (for example, teams hiring for internal-tools or automation roles).
3. Keep CRM fields current on a schedule so comp and territory models run on clean data.

## Linear (Marcus Chen, Head of Growth)
**Signals (all verified, from Linear's own 2026-08-26 post unless noted)**
- $99M employee tender at a $2.5B valuation. Cash-flow positive. Passed $100M ARR. 40,000+ paying companies. Net revenue retention 177%.
- Salesforce and Figma became customers in 2026. OpenAI, Cursor, Cognition, Harvey and others run product work in Linear.

- Loops (recurring agent workflows) launched 2026-07-20 on Business and Enterprise plans. Linear Agent launched 2026-03-24.
- 30+ open roles, including sales.

**Likely problems (proposed)**
- With 177% NRR, the growth is expansion inside existing accounts. Knowing which workspaces are about to expand, or are ready for Business or Enterprise, is the main lever.
- Big-company adoption often starts organically (Salesforce did), so the champion is hard to see.
- Self-serve signup volume with a growing sales team needs a clean handoff.

**Use cases (proposed)**
1. Spot workspaces that fit Business or Enterprise (headcount growth, security or compliance hiring, multiple teams on Linear) and route them to sales.
2. Find the champion and the buying committee at a company that adopted Linear bottom-up.
3. Trigger a play when an account shows a signal (new engineering leader, funding, AI-team hiring).

**Caution:** the 2026-09-29 CLAUDE.md note about a $2.5B valuation came from a secondary source. It is now confirmed by Linear's own post.

3. Prioritize accounts by a score so SDRs work the top of the list first.

**Caution:** Loom and Atlassian may be outside the ICP's 50-500 employee band. The page should not mention the seat-removal backlash as a criticism of the prospect's own company.
